Privacy Policy

Layho is a live speech translation app. This policy describes how Layho accesses, uses, stores, and shares information, including Google user data, when you use the app.

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Contact us

You can contact us about privacy at support@layho.app.

Google user data we access

When you choose Sign in with Google, Layho requests only these Google scopes:

  • openid — to verify your Google account
  • email — your Google email address
  • profile — your Google account name and basic profile information

From Google, we receive an ID token that can include your Google account identifier, email address, name, and profile picture. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar, or any other Google product data.

How we use Google user data

We use Google user data only to provide and improve Layho’s user-facing features:

  • to sign you in and confirm you are authorized to use the app
  • to show your name on the home screen
  • to obtain a speech-translation service key for your session

We do not use Google user data for advertising, targeted ads, retargeting, or interest-based ads. We do not sell Google user data. We do not use Google user data to determine credit-worthiness, for lending, to build marketing databases, or to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.

How we store Google user data

After you sign in, Layho stores your Google ID token in a cookie on your device so you can stay signed in. That cookie expires after 7 days. During sign-in we also keep short-lived values in your browser to complete the Google sign-in flow; those values are removed when sign-in finishes or the browser session ends.

We send the ID token to our login backend, hosted on Amazon Web Services, so we can verify your identity and return your display name and a key for the speech translation service.

How we share Google user data

We share Google user data only as needed to operate Layho:

  • Google — to authenticate you when you sign in
  • Amazon Web Services — our login backend receives your Google ID token to verify your account and issue a speech-translation key

We do not sell, rent, or transfer Google user data to data brokers, information resellers, advertisers, or other third parties for their own use. We do not send your Google name, email, or ID token to Microsoft Azure. If we are required by law to disclose information, we will do so only to the extent required.

Data protection

We take steps to protect Google user data and other information used by Layho:

  • sign-in uses Google Identity Services over HTTPS
  • the sign-in cookie is marked Secure and SameSite when the app is served over HTTPS
  • the Google ID token is sent to our login backend over HTTPS
  • speech audio is sent to Microsoft Azure Speech over a secure connection

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please protect access to your device and your Google account.

Retention and deletion

The sign-in cookie is kept for up to 7 days, or until you sign out, whichever comes first. Signing out deletes that cookie from your browser and ends your session.

Language and playback preferences are stored in a separate cookie on your device for up to 1 year. You can remove it by clearing cookies for this site in your browser.

To request deletion of personal information we may hold (for example, a waitlist sign-up), email support@layho.app. We will delete or destroy that information unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Signing out of Layho does not by itself delete a waitlist request you already submitted.

Other information Layho collects

Microphone and speech

If you start a translation session, Layho uses your microphone to capture speech. Audio is sent to Microsoft Azure Speech (West US) for speech recognition, translation, and, if you enable voice output, synthesized speech. Transcripts and translations are shown in your browser for the current session. Layho does not save transcripts or audio files in your browser after you leave the session.

Microsoft processes this audio according to its own terms and privacy statement.

Waitlist sign-up

If you are not signed in and submit the Sign up form, we collect the name and email address you enter so we can contact you about access. That form is submitted through Web3Forms. Web3Forms processes the submission according to its own privacy policy.

Settings

We store your chosen source and target languages, voice-output preference, and conversation-mode preference in a cookie on your device so those choices persist between visits.

Third-party services

Layho uses these services to provide the app:

  • Google — sign-in
  • Amazon Web Services — login backend
  • Microsoft Azure Speech — speech recognition, translation, and optional voice output
  • Web3Forms — waitlist form delivery

Those providers process data under their own policies when you use the related features.

Cookies

Layho uses cookies and similar browser storage to keep you signed in, remember your language and feature preferences, and complete Google sign-in. You can delete them by signing out and by clearing cookies for this site in your browser.

Children

Layho is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Layho uses Google user data, we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date above. Where required, we will ask you to consent before using Google user data in a new way.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests to access or delete your information: support@layho.app.
