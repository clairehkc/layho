Getting started

Layho translates speech in real time.

  1. In Settings, choose the language you speak and the language to translate into.
  2. Tap Start, then tap the microphone button (or press S) and speak.

Voice reads the translation aloud. Conversation listens for both languages and switches automatically. Transcription only shows what was said without translating. Conversation and Transcription only can't be used at the same time, and neither can Voice and Conversation.

You can open this guide anytime from Settings.
